# Website sources

- Patch-note content: `../data/retail-patch-notes.json`, maintained through the
  repository's Blizzard-first refresh workflow.
- Class icons: Blizzard Entertainment's World of Warcraft render service at
  `https://render.worldofwarcraft.com/us/icons/56/`.
- Manrope and Sora fonts: packaged locally through Fontsource; no visitor data
  is sent to a third-party font host.

Source and provenance details are for maintainers. They are intentionally not
shown inside the player-facing patch-note interface.
